\section{Implicit ALE}

On top of implicit. \code{examples/indenter/implicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Restricted adaptive domain}

ALE only accepts first-order reduced-integration solids (\texttt{C3D8R}).
An empty set is silent. Standard has no initial mesh sweeps.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=175,lastline=192,firstnumber=175]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/implicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Diagnostics after \texttt{*Static}}

CAE cannot write \texttt{*Diagnostics}. Without it you cannot prove ALE ran.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=204,lastline=205,firstnumber=204]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/implicit_ale/model.py}

\section{Explicit}

On top of implicit, replacing the solver. \code{examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Mass (\texttt{*Density})}

Required. The stable increment is $L_{\min}/c_d$.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=85,lastline=87,firstnumber=85]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Explicit element library}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=101,lastline=102,firstnumber=101]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Dynamic explicit step, not static}

Drop automatic stabilisation. Bulk viscosity is on by default (\texttt{ALLVD}).
Do not add extra damping to hide kinetic energy.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=125,lastline=133,firstnumber=125]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Energy history}

Peak \texttt{ALLKE}/\texttt{ALLIE} below about 5~\% is acceptable, below 1~\%
is quotable.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=134,lastline=138,firstnumber=134]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Velocity control, not displacement}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=163,lastline=167,firstnumber=163]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Explicit general contact}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=175,lastline=178,firstnumber=175]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Double precision job}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=186,lastline=191,firstnumber=186]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Optional mass scaling}

Commented at the bottom of the Explicit script. Semi-automatic, target
$\Delta t$. Then re-check \texttt{ALLKE}.

\subsubsection*{Optional section controls}

Also commented. Distortion control or enhanced hourglass, not both.
CAE cannot write \texttt{*Section Controls}.

\section{Explicit CEL}

On top of explicit, replacing the Lagrangian soil.
\code{examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Eulerian part with void above the soil}

Do not set the void to zero. Heave needs empty elements.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=70,lastline=83,firstnumber=70]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Eulerian section and \texttt{EC3D8R}}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=99,lastline=111,firstnumber=99]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Initial volume fraction}

Cells below $z=0$ start full. This is
\texttt{*Initial Conditions, type=VOLUME FRACTION}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=131,lastline=141,firstnumber=131]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Walls, free top}

Zero normal motion on the sides and base. The top is open.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=173,lastline=176,firstnumber=173]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{EVF on the field output}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=169,lastline=171,firstnumber=169]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{No geostatic, no ALE}

\texttt{*Geostatic} has no Eulerian implementation. General contact is
mandatory. There is no \texttt{*Adaptive Mesh} block.
