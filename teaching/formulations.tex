\noindent
Each section lists what you must \emph{add or change} relative to the previous
rung. Geometry, rollers, the rigid indenter and the soil block stay put until
CEL. Shared builder: \coderef{examples/common/indenter_model.py}{99}.

\section{Implicit}

The Standard baseline. \code{examples/indenter/implicit}.

\subsubsection*{Geometrically nonlinear static step}

Without \texttt{nlgeom} there is no large displacement. Automatic stabilisation
is fictitious viscous damping. It is on because the analysis otherwise stops
as soon as the soil fails. Check \texttt{ALLSD} against \texttt{ALLIE}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=53,lastline=63,firstnumber=53]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\subsubsection*{Displacement control}

The indenter is driven by a prescribed displacement on the rigid-body
reference point, ramped with a tabular amplitude over a unit time period.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=199,lastline=203,firstnumber=199]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=228,lastline=232,firstnumber=228]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\subsubsection*{Standard element library}

Same \texttt{C3D8R} topology as Explicit. The library flag must be
\texttt{STANDARD}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=150,lastline=153,firstnumber=150]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\subsubsection*{Standard general contact}

\code{ContactStd}, not the Explicit interaction.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=108,lastline=114,firstnumber=108]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/contact.py}

\subsubsection*{Standard job}

No \texttt{DOUBLE\_PLUS\_PACK}. Thread parallelisation, not Explicit domains.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=84,lastline=93,firstnumber=84]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/jobs.py}

\subsubsection*{Optional geostatic}

Self-weight belongs in a \texttt{*Geostatic} step, then import into later
steps. The teaching indenters skip this. A quantitative model must not.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=100,lastline=101,firstnumber=100]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\section{Implicit ALE}

On top of implicit. \code{examples/indenter/implicit_ale}.

\subsubsection*{Reduced-integration first-order solids}

ALE domains may contain only \texttt{C3D8R} (or \texttt{CAX4R},
\texttt{CPE4R}). Other types in the set are silently nonadaptive. The implicit
model already uses \texttt{C3D8R}. Do not switch to fully integrated or
second-order elements.

\subsubsection*{Adaptive mesh controls and a restricted set}

A box written in absolute coordinates is the usual way to get an empty, inert
domain. Print what the set actually contains.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=51,lastline=58,firstnumber=51]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/implicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{No initial mesh sweeps}

Abaqus/Standard has none. Pass \texttt{None} so the keyword is omitted.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=242,lastline=250,firstnumber=242]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/ale.py}

\subsubsection*{Diagnostics after \texttt{*Static}}

CAE cannot write \texttt{*Diagnostics}. Inject it or you cannot prove the
domain swept. Standard may still omit the Explicit-style \texttt{.msg} block.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=63,lastline=64,firstnumber=63]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/implicit_ale/model.py}

Standard ALE is Lagrangian-only, one smoothing algorithm, intended for wear
and acoustics. Do not expect skirt penetration from it.

\section{Explicit}

On top of implicit, replacing the solver. \code{examples/indenter/explicit}.
This is the largest jump.

\subsubsection*{Mass (\texttt{*Density})}

Explicit cannot run without density. The stable increment is
$L_{\min}/c_d$ and $c_d$ depends on $\rho$. A Standard model that never had
\texttt{*Density} will not data-check. This repository always writes it.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=69,lastline=72,firstnumber=69]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/materials.py}

\subsubsection*{Explicit element library}

Same \texttt{C3D8R} code, \texttt{EXPLICIT} library. Mixing libraries in one
part fails at data check.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=150,lastline=153,firstnumber=150]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\subsubsection*{Dynamic explicit step, not static}

Drop \texttt{*Static} and automatic stabilisation. Bulk viscosity is on by
default on \texttt{*Dynamic, Explicit} (watch \texttt{ALLVD}). Do not add
Rayleigh or material damping to hide kinetic energy. Slow the rate or scale
less mass instead. See \code{docs/01-explicit-quasi-static.md}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=79,lastline=84,firstnumber=79]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\subsubsection*{Time period from rate, not a unit interval}

The step length is penetration depth divided by the prescribed velocity. That
velocity is scaled far above reality. Print the increment count before you
submit.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=193,lastline=197,firstnumber=193]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\subsubsection*{Smooth-step amplitude}

A step change of velocity puts a stress wave through the model on increment 1.
\texttt{SMOOTH STEP} ramps over the first few percent of the step.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=152,lastline=155,firstnumber=152]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\subsubsection*{Velocity control, not displacement}

A constant rate makes the resistance curve interpretable. Sign is negative
because penetration is $-z$.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=222,lastline=226,firstnumber=222]{\reporoot/examples/common/indenter_model.py}

\subsubsection*{Explicit general contact}

\code{ContactExp}. Penalty friction. Mandatory later for CEL, and the right
default here because the contact surface changes as the soil folds.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=81,lastline=86,firstnumber=81]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/contact.py}

\subsubsection*{Double precision job and domain parallel}

Single precision corrupts a long soil run. Loop-level parallelisation is not
available on Windows. \texttt{numDomains} must be a multiple of
\texttt{numCpus}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=63,lastline=70,firstnumber=63]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/jobs.py}

\subsubsection*{Energy history}

Request it. Grade the peak \texttt{ALLKE}/\texttt{ALLIE}, not the final value.
Below about 5~\% is acceptable, below 1~\% is quotable.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=172,lastline=176,firstnumber=172]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\subsubsection*{Optional mass scaling}

Semi-automatic, target $\Delta t$, throughout the step. It scales the worst
elements, which in a penetration run are next to the structure. Check added
mass in the \texttt{.sta} and re-check \texttt{ALLKE}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=128,lastline=135,firstnumber=128]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/steps.py}

\subsubsection*{Optional section controls}

Distortion control or enhanced hourglass, not both. CAE in 2021 has no API.
Inject after \texttt{*Preprint} and attach to the soil \texttt{*Solid Section}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=70,lastline=73,firstnumber=70]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit/model.py}

\subsubsection*{UMAT becomes VUMAT}

If the soil is a user material, the Explicit job needs a VUMAT (or a wrapper),
the matching \texttt{*Depvar} count, and initialised state. Verify on
\code{constitutive/single_element} first.

\section{Explicit ALE}

On top of explicit. \code{examples/indenter/explicit_ale}. Keep everything
from the previous section.

\subsubsection*{Adaptive mesh controls}

\lstinputlisting[firstline=65,lastline=65,firstnumber=65]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Restricted element set}

Do not make the whole part adaptive. A box around the indenter is the
production configuration. An empty set is an inert domain with no warning.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=66,lastline=74,firstnumber=66]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Initial mesh sweeps}

Explicit has them. Default in CAE is 5 at the start of the step, then
\texttt{frequency=10} and \texttt{mesh sweeps=1}. The keyword reference that
says default sweeps is 5 is wrong. See \code{docs/02-ale-adaptive-meshing.md}.

\subsubsection*{Diagnostics, then prove it ran}

Inject \texttt{*Diagnostics, adaptive mesh=summary} after
\texttt{*Dynamic, Explicit}. Then read the \texttt{.msg}. Zero percent of nodes
moved means the domain was defined and did nothing.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=79,lastline=80,firstnumber=79]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_ale/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Parallel domains}

Nodes shared between Explicit domains are nonadaptive. For a study of ALE,
run one CPU. For production, several well-separated regions. Adjacent regions
that share a face are treated as one. See
\code{docs/03-ale-multi-region-parallel.md}.

\subsubsection*{Still not a remesh}

Topology is fixed. When material must flow around a tip and close behind it,
ALE stops helping. That is CEL.

\section{Explicit CEL}

On top of explicit, replacing the Lagrangian soil. \code{examples/indenter/explicit_cel}.
Keep density, the Explicit step, smooth-step velocity drive, Explicit contact,
double precision and energy output. Drop ALE.

\subsubsection*{Eulerian part with void above the soil}

The mesh is a fixed box of space. Heave needs empty elements. No void means
resistance is silently too high.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=64,lastline=73,firstnumber=64]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Eulerian elements}

\texttt{EC3D8R}, Explicit library, structured hex. A swept or free mesh is
rejected.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=94,lastline=99,firstnumber=94]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/cel.py}

\subsubsection*{Eulerian section, not a solid section}

The soil section is \texttt{*Eulerian Section}. The indenter stays a
Lagrangian solid.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=55,lastline=57,firstnumber=55]{\reporoot/lib/fldlib/cel.py}

\subsubsection*{Initial volume fraction}

Cells below $z=0$ start full. Everything else starts empty. This is
\texttt{*Initial Conditions, type=VOLUME FRACTION}.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=126,lastline=132,firstnumber=126]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{Walls, not rollers, and a free top}

Zero normal displacement on the four sides and the base so material cannot
leave the box. The top is open. Closing it is another way to lose heave.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=169,lastline=176,firstnumber=169]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{No geostatic}

\texttt{*Geostatic} has no Eulerian implementation. Initialise stress directly
or settle dynamically (slow, and it rings).

\subsubsection*{EVF on the field output}

Without Eulerian volume fraction you cannot see where the material is.

\lstinputlisting[firstline=150,lastline=153,firstnumber=150]{\reporoot/examples/indenter/explicit_cel/model.py}

\subsubsection*{General contact is mandatory}

The Eulerian--Lagrangian interaction exists only through general contact.
There is no ALE block. The free surface is one element thick.
